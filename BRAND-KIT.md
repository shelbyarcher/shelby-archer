## 2) Package menu (v1 pricing)

Grounded in 2026 indie-pro marketplace ranges. Your credits support **mid-to-upper indie**, not bottom-tier. Isabella-style $2k/song production stays a separate custom quote.

### Singles
| Package | What's included | Price (USD) | Turnaround |
|---|---|---:|---|
| **Master only** | Streaming-ready master, 2 revision rounds, loudness + format suite | **$125**/song | 2–4 days |
| **Mix** | Full mix from stems, 2 revision rounds, stereo + instrumental | **$550**/song | 5–7 days |
| **Mix + Master** | Mix + master as one pass, 2 revision rounds | **$650**/song | 7–10 days |
| **Production + Mix** (custom) | Arrangement/production help + mix | from **$1,500**/song | scoped |

### Bundles
| Bundle | Pricing |
|---|---|
| EP mix (4–6 songs) | 10% off single mix rate |
| EP mix + master | 12% off |
| Album mix (8–12 songs) | 15% off |
| Album master only | $100/song (10+ songs) |

### Add-ons
- Extra revision round: $50
- Vocal tuning / comp cleanup: from $75/song
- Rush (48–72 hr mix): +40%
- Alt mix (radio / band / acoustic): $100
- Stem master (instead of stereo): +$50

### Deposit / terms (put on site)
- 50% to start, 50% before final files
- 2 revisions included; then hourly or per-round
- Files: WAV 24-bit + streaming masters
- Cancel before work starts: deposit refund minus $50 admin; after first draft: deposit kept

**Why these numbers:** Marketplace mix listings often sit $200–$500; solid indie-pro is $200–$700 mix and $75–$150 master. You undercut "famous engineer" rates but sit above portfolio-builders — correct for Sound City + label history.

## 6) Outreach system (outside word of mouth)

### Weekly cadence (3–4 focused hours)
| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| Mon | 30 min: update marketplace messages; post 1 before/after or mix tip |
| Tue | 45 min: 5 personal outreach notes (artists) |
| Wed | 30 min: follow up open threads; deliver work |
| Thu | 45 min: 5 more notes OR 2 partner emails (engineers/managers) |
| Fri | 20 min: pipeline review — quotes out, deposits in, next week's slots |

Target: **10 artist notes + 2 partner touches / week**. Consistency beats blasts.

### Who to contact
**A. Artists (primary)**
- Indie / alt / singer-songwriter with unreleased singles
- Find via: Spotify "Release Radar" style new releases that sound under-mixed, Bandcamp new, IG #indiemusic, local LA openers, Songkick/bandsintown small venues

**B. Partners (leverage)**
- Tracking engineers who don't mix
- Vocal producers / tuners
- Indie managers / small labels
- Photographers / video people around Sound City who meet artists

### Artist outreach script (IG DM / email)
Hey [Name] — I'm Shelby, mixer/mastering eng at Sound City in Van Nuys. Heard [Song] — [one specific true compliment].

If you want a denser / clearer / more competitive version for the next single, I take remote stems. Mix is $550, master $125, mix+master $650. Happy to listen to a private link and tell you straight whether I'm the right fit.

Site/profile: [link]

No pressure either way — just fuck with the song.

### Partner outreach script
Hey [Name] — Shelby Archer here. I mix and master out of Sound City Center. If you ever track artists who need a mix/master handoff, I'm taking remote work and can be a clean referral (I don't poach tracking).

One-pager + samples: [link]  
I also leave room in the budget so your client isn't surprised.

Appreciate you —

### Soft "sample" rule (don't give free forever)
If someone is hot but unsure: offer a **paid mini** — e.g. master one song at full rate, or a paid mix sample at $275 for a rough first pass on one track credited toward full mix if they continue. Avoid unpaid "trial mixes."

### Pipeline tracking (simple Sheet columns)
Date | Source (SB/AirGigs/IG/Partner/WOM) | Name | Service | $ Quote | Status (new/quoted/deposit/in progress/done) | Next step | Review asked?

### 30-day success metrics
- Profiles live on SoundBetter + AirGigs
- Site one-pager live (Carrd / Framer / Squarespace — whatever is fastest)
- 20 outreach notes sent
- 5+ serious replies
- 2+ paid jobs from non-WOM sources
- 3 marketplace reviews in motion
